Me Jean Fédol MAMBOU KOAGNE

With the firm since 2016, trainee attorney since 2024

With the firm since 2016 and a trainee attorney since 2024, Me Jean Fédol Koagne Mambou has built, over the years, a practice as broad as it is solid, at the crossroads of business law and public law.

  • With the firmSince 2016
  • Trainee AttorneySince 2024
  • ResearchDoctorate in law

(01)Background

His practice extends from private law to business law, taking in civil law, administrative law and land law, with particular expertise in administrative litigation — fields he is deepening today through his doctorate in law, at the meeting point of research and practice. This generalist expertise is complemented by specialized certifications in insurance law, financial markets law and intellectual property law, enabling him to assist clients on matters with high technical and financial stakes.

Exacting and methodical, he places this rare versatility, between public law and business law, at the service of a single ambition: to offer every client a precise legal answer, whatever the ground on which their case is played out.
